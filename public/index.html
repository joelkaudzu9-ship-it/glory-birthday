"I thank my God every time I remember you."

When paths first crossed

I still remember the first day we met on those corridors. We were both stepping into a new chapter, carrying our own fears, questions, and uncertainties.

There were so many unknowns ahead of us. But looking back now, it amazes me how God can take simple moments and turn them into meaningful relationships.

What started as conversations during a season of adjustment grew into something I truly value — a friendship built on encouragement, faith, and genuine care.
🌱

Growing deeper

Over time, our conversations went beyond just school. We've talked about faith, purpose, struggles, doubts, and dreams.

And through those conversations, I've seen the kind of person you are — someone who genuinely desires to grow, someone who seeks God, and someone who cares deeply about the people around her.

Glory, I want you to know that I thank God for you. You've been someone I can turn to during moments of confusion, uncertainty, and doubt.
💛

A heart that celebrates

I especially appreciate the many times you've asked me, "Are you okay?" It might seem like a small question, but sometimes small questions carry great love behind them.

And there's something else about you that I really admire: you have a beautiful ability to celebrate others.

I cannot count the times you've been there cheering me on... You don't just watch people succeed from a distance — you celebrate with them.

In a world where people can sometimes compare or compete, having someone who sincerely rejoices when others shine is a rare gift.

🌟

Pursuing what matters

Seeing the person you're becoming brings so much joy to my heart. Seeing you serve so diligently in church, giving yourself to God's work with commitment and love, inspires me.

Your dedication in academics and being recognized on the Dean's List is also something to celebrate. But beyond achievements, what makes me rejoice the most is seeing God working in your life.

It's beautiful to see someone who is not only pursuing excellence but also pursuing God. Your life is a reminder that success is not only about what we achieve but also about who we become.
🙏

A prayer for you

As you begin another year, my prayer is that God continues to guide every step you take. May He deepen your relationship with Him, strengthen your faith, and give you wisdom for every decision ahead.

May He expand your territory, bless the work of your hands, and open doors prepared specifically for you. May your service to Him continue bearing fruit.

May this year bring you greater joy, greater purpose, greater peace, and countless reminders that you are loved by God and that your life has meaning.

wherever it flows.

of the life God is shaping in you —
not a life built only on moments of applause,
but one built in the secret places,
where character is formed,
where faith grows,
where hearts learn to serve.
